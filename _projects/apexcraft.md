### **MUP-Bank** 
The MUP-Bank is a modular, universal powerbank system designed to overcome the
limitations of traditional single-purpose powerbanks. Current mobile power solutions lack
flexibility, offer limited performance, and require users to purchase multiple devices for
different scenarios. The MUP-Bank addresses these challenges through stackable
magnetic modules, scalable battery capacity, modern fast-charging technology, and smart
connectivity features. Over the course of the project, the team developed a functional
prototype with integrated battery management, USB-PD/PPS support, WiFi-based
monitoring, a local TFT user interface, and a durable, user-centered housing design. The
solution adapts to everyday users, students, and professionals alike by providing
customizable configurations without technical complexity. The project demonstrates that
modularity, transparency, and portability can be combined into a single intuitive system,
paving the way for future expansions such as solar charging, Bluetooth connectivity, and
additional functional modules.
