The <a href="https://www.tech2x.eu">Technology for innovation and entrepreneurship excellence (TECH2X)</a> project aims to boost the innovation and entrepreneurial capacity of the consortium’s HEIs to foster deep techtalent and respond to the need to transition towards the 4th generation university model. Through practice-based collaborations both within, between and beyond our institutions focusing on the areas of climate and mobility, we intend to create a scalable initiative for excellent science-based innovation.
