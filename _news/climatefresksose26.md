From climate knowledge to climate action at inno.space of Hochschule Mannheim! 

How can one take complex scientific data and turn it into real-world impact? Recently, 12 students from across our faculties took on this challenge during our university's first Climate Fresk workshop.

This internationally recognized, interactive format developed from Climate Fresk simplifies the complex cause-and-effect chains of climate change using IPCC-backed data. But it doesn't stop at the facts - students brainstormed concrete, sustainable ideas for both campus life and their future professional careers.

What’s next? Thanks to our 6 certified internal facilitators, Hochschule Mannheim will now offer at least two Climate Fresk workshops every semester to their students. We are proud to strengthen ecological responsibility and drive the conversation on societal transformation.

Thanks to Jochen Klemm from Climate Fresk Deutschland for your engagement in the region and Manuel Walter, Peter Kaiser and Kirstin Kohler for facilitating the workshop.
