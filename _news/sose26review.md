The summer semester ended a month ago, but before the next one begins, we wanted to take a moment to look back on our summer semester...
We successfully ran two courses — our beloved PDP and TDX — and brought the second half of our CBI programme to a strong finish.

We also attended the osapiens Sustainability Summit in June, where we had the opportunity to listen to Dr. Robert Habeck speak. Let’s just say we were a little starstruck.

We also moderated two Climate Fresks, raising awareness of how our everyday decisions impact the climate and contribute to a more sustainable future.
Last but not least, we welcomed our newest T-Team member, Laurin, in June. He had an exciting start, joining us for the i.EXPO and the final presentations of our courses.

All in all, it was a very busy and eventful end to another semester. We wish all our alumni students continued success and hope everyone is enjoying a restful summer.
